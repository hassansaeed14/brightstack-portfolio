UI/UX design · mobile app

Redesigning medication reminders for people managing multiple prescriptions

A concept app that helps people track daily medications without the anxiety of "did I already take that?" — designed end to end, from problem framing to final screens.

Role
UI/UX Designer
Scope
Research → Wireframes → UI
Tools
Figma, user interviews
Timeline
3 weeks, concept project

The problem

People managing 3+ daily medications lose track of what's taken and what's due — especially older adults and caregivers managing prescriptions for family members.

Cognitive load

Existing apps show too much information at once — full schedules, dosage charts, refill dates — all competing for attention on one screen.

No clear "right now" answer

Users couldn't quickly answer "what do I need to take right now" — the single question that mattered most in the moment.

The screens

Three core screens: today's view, a completed state, and adherence tracking — designed so the most urgent information is always the largest thing on screen.

Design principles

One question per screen

Each screen answers exactly one question: what's due, what's done, or how's the week going.

Color means urgency, not decoration

Coral only appears on things still due — once taken, everything shifts to a calm neutral tone.

One-tap completion

The single most common action — marking a dose taken — never takes more than one tap from the home screen.
