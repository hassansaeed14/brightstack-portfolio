01 — The Research & Problem Domain

Standard travel booking flows leak up to 42% of high-intent traffic due to perceived API latency and hidden ancillary surcharges introduced at checkout.

Friction Point A

The Search Latency Blackout

Full-screen generic loaders trigger user anxiety and repeated double-taps, causing accidental server load and premature session bounce.

"If the screen freezes for two seconds without visual feedback, I assume failure."
Friction Point B

Surprise Baggage Surcharges

Revealing mandatory baggage fees only at the final payment screen destroys brand trust and inflates cart abandonment.

"The final price doubled after adding basic luggage at checkout."
Design Principle 1

Anticipatory Shimmer Layouts

Deploy structural placeholders mirroring incoming result cards, cutting perceived wait times by half.

Design Principle 2

Upfront Transparent Tiering

Integrate baggage and seat tiers early in the selection loop to lock price transparency from step one.

03 — Usability Validation & Metrics

Post-launch cohort analysis across 500 active beta users over 30 days.

01
Zero Duplicate Searches

Skeleton shimmers completely eliminated user re-tap behavior during search latency windows, dropping duplicate backend calls to 0%.

02
18% Higher Ancillary Conversion

Exposing baggage pricing upfront during seat selection rather than at the final payment screen removed sticker shock and increased bag add-on uptake.

03
100% Seat Selection Accuracy

High-contrast selected states with electric fuchsia glows eradicated user complaints regarding incorrect auto-assigned seating.
