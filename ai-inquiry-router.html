Pipeline architecture

A lightweight, transparent processing stream designed for absolute predictability and fast debugging.

Built as an inspectable rule-based classifier for transparent demonstration. For enterprise scale, this identical pipeline schema can easily interface with fine-tuned transformer models once historical dataset volume permits.

Limitations and production roadmap

  • Keyword dependency — relies on predefined term arrays and may miss indirect semantic phrasing.
  • English localization — current scoring matrices are optimized strictly for English syntax.
  • Sample baseline — validated on 60 test messages; edge-case coverage scales with larger training corpora.
  • Next phase — integrate historical ticket logs to train a lightweight BERT classifier while retaining the same fallback rules.
