// ABOUT

A small studio that designs and builds.

BrightStack Co. is a digital studio based in Mansehra, Pakistan, working since 2024. We design and build websites, web apps, UI/UX and practical AI tools, from the first sketch to the live site.

How we work

  1. 01Talk

    You tell us what you need. We agree on the scope and the price before any work starts.

  2. 02Design

    We plan the layout and the look in Figma, and you approve it.

  3. 03Build

    We build the site or app and share progress as we go.

  4. 04Launch

    Final changes, then it goes live and we hand it over.
