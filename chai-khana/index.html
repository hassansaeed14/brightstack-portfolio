Our Story

From a Small Dhaba to Karachi's Premium Spot

In 2019, Chai Khana started as a humble roadside dhaba with just two kettles and a dream to serve the absolute best tea in town. Today, we have evolved into a premium modern desi café in the heart of Gulshan-e-Iqbal, serving hundreds of families and students every evening.

Whether you're looking to share a quiet evening with family or catch up with friends over hot, crispy parathas and strong Karak Chai, Chai Khana provides the perfect cosy, modern, and hygienic environment.
