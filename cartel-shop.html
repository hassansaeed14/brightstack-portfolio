Cartel — Shopping & Checkout

A mobile shopping app prototype built to sell the moment that actually loses buyers — checkout. Every tap gives feedback: the cart badge pops when an item is added, swiping a cart row removes it with a real animation, and the step tracker fills in as you move through checkout instead of just labeling the current step.

The details that build checkout confidence

Nothing here is decorative — every animation exists to answer a real question a shopper has mid-purchase: did that register? is this reversible? how much further do I have to go?

↳ tap a product, add it to the bag, then open the cart
1

Popping cart badge. Adding an item makes the badge visibly bounce, not just increment silently — confirming the tap actually did something.

2

Swipe-out removal. Removing a cart item slides it out and collapses the space it left, rather than having it vanish and jump the layout.

3

Filling step tracker. The checkout progress bar fills between steps instead of just relabeling "Step 2 of 3" — visible progress reduces the anxiety of a multi-step form.
