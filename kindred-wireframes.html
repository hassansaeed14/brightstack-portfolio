UX Philosophy: "The Gesture is the Interface"

In high-frequency discovery apps, traditional tap targets are minimized in favor of direct manipulation. Wireframing these flows requires functional physics from the earliest stages.

Rotational Kinematics: Cards tilt proportionally to horizontal drag displacement for realistic inertia.
Threshold Feedback: LIKE/PASS stamps scale opacity smoothly to signal commit boundaries.
State Transition: Cyclic card evaluation triggers match overlay states to test full user journey loops.
Figma Delivery: Constructed with Smart Animate and drag triggers for developer handover.
