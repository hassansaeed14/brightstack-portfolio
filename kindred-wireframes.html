Discovery. Cards stack three-deep so the "more coming" affordance is visible without needing a scroll hint.

Direction: "the gesture is the interface"

Almost nothing on the discovery screen is a traditional tap target — the card itself is the control. Wireframing that means the drag physics have to exist even at this fidelity, or the concept can't actually be evaluated.

Rotation follows drag. The card tilts proportionally to horizontal drag distance — the same subtle physics a built app would use, not just a straight slide.
Stamp opacity as a threshold cue. LIKE/PASS stamps fade in with drag distance, giving a visual read on how far is "far enough" before release.
Match overlay reachable. One of the four cards is scripted to trigger a match state, so that screen exists as a real wireframe, not just a mention in the brief.
Figma file. Delivered with the same drag interaction built via Smart Animate + drag triggers, not flattened images.
