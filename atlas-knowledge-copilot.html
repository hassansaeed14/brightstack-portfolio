Retrieval here runs a lightweight local relevance scoring pass over the knowledge base, then a local extractive step pulls the most relevant sentence from each top-matching passage and cites it directly — fully deterministic, no external API call. In a client deployment this scales to a real vector database (Pinecone, Weaviate, or pgvector) for retrieval and a connected LLM for generation — the grounding and citation behavior stays exactly the same.
