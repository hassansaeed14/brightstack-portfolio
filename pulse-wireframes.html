What the concept covers
Six ideas the concept explores. None of them is a working product.
01 / 06
Brainwave view

Shows alpha and theta waves as a simple sign of how relaxed you are. Simulated here.

02 / 06
Warmer colours at night

The app shifts to warmer colours after sunset.

03 / 06
Wellness summary export

Puts your breathing history into a short summary you could share.

04 / 06
Shared breathing rooms

Breathe in time with other people in the same room.

05 / 06
On-device data

The idea is to keep journal notes and history on the phone only.

06 / 06
3D soundscapes

Background sounds such as rain or a mountain valley, placed around you.
